Once an esteemed writer asked me if I’m a spy, sitting at Veselka. _I Want to Show You More_, the title of a professor's book is also befitting. EMK is the advance-planner of a work; anything spied upon is first seen as the makings of a story. Sometimes I read from the story at an open-mic.

EMK is a name I’ve made for myself whose backstory spans NYC, Mexico City, Cairo, São Paulo and Tennessee. It started as minor graffiti, stickers co-designed with artist friends: Adham Bakry (Egypt), Gustavo Marcasse (Brazil), and Jakub Szczęsny (Poland). Besides sticking stickers, EMK writes and performs interventions.

I've blogged before as the [Artful Administrator](https://artfuladministrator.wordpress.com/) and once in a short dialogue [with Canadian artist, Jacob Wren.](https://medium.com/@toddlester_75142/tangentially-yours-a-conversation-series-with-jacob-wren-1-f798120c8a3a)

#Eu❤️Cazuza is somehow a new page, and 🥋 a new playbook.
