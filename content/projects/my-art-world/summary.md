Ernst Bloch suggested that the purpose of utopic experiments–world building as such–is to engage with concrete historical struggles. In a chapter for [AIDS Sem Capa: reflexões virais sobre um mundo pós-pandemia](https://tllester.info/pdfs/Aids_Sem_Capa_ToddLanierLester_edit.pdf) (2022), I say:

_NASA proudly landed a spacecraft on Mars early in Biden’s 1st term, yet throughout the history of space travel there has been no credible sighting of the art world and many haven’t even heard of it. The outcome I’ve been asked to pursue is therefore complicated not by a majority wanting confirmation the art world exists, but by one not caring whether its existence is proven or debunked, simply not knowing to care. Given that it might all be a hoax, the findings–I was told–should be useful in other arenas._

The title of the book suggests a world without HIV/AIDS; my line, a manner of teasing at the role of arts and activism surveyed by the text. After a few of these experiences, it seems that to evoke a world, an artist must help build it. I attempted this a few times with different ideas, starting in earnest while living in Khartoum and launching freeDimensional, an effort for artist safety.
